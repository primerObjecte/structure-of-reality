## Thesis

The world is an **uneven space of possible encounters**.  
Objects are temporary crystallizations of gaps, arising under three conditions:

- **Density** — connectedness with other gaps  
- **Flow** — energy, information, matter passing through the gap  
- **Material** — a carrier capable of taking form

Crystallization generates new gaps. Decrystallization has different modes (sleep, fainting, death — qualitatively distinct processes).

---

## Formalization (Category Theory)

**Gap** = a non-existent limit of a diagram D: J → C in a category C, characterized by its universal property in an extension C' ⊃ C.

**Density of a gap** = |Ob(J)| + |Mor(J)| — the size of the indexing category J.

**Flow through a gap** = |{ f ∈ Mor(C) : f factors through the gap in C' }|.

**Material** = dimension of the state space / degrees of freedom of the system.

**Crystallization** = the appearance of the limit lim D in C. The new object is the crystallized gap.

## Evidence Across Six Levels

### 1. Mathematics
Category theory shows that objects are defined through relations (morphisms). Non-existent limits are gaps that crystallize into new objects when the category is extended.

### 2. Biology
A niche is a stable gap in an ecosystem. A species is the crystallization of this gap.  
**Prediction:** Post-extinction radiations should show directionality toward attractors, not random expansion.  
**Data:** Convergent evolution (dolphin, ichthyosaur, shark) — one gap crystallized twice.  
**Status:** Partially confirmed — directionality exists, but there is a phase of randomness.

### 3. Neuroscience
Consciousness is a gap that became its own reflector (a loop).  
**Distinction:** A system that merely integrates (complex filter) vs a system that integrates while changing the conditions of its own next integration (a loop).  
**Data:** Three conditions of crystallization in the brain: density (hub regions), flow (gathering → integration → broadcast), material (plasticity).  
**Decrystallization:** sleep (reconfiguration), fainting (flow collapse), death (material destruction) — qualitatively different modes.

### 4. Sociology
Social norms are crystallizations of gaps in interaction networks.  
**Data:** Agents change behavior when they see ~25% of others have changed. Network density lowers the threshold.  
**Independent convergence:** Sociologists arrived at the same structure (threshold, network, flow) without our language.

### 5. Origin of Life
Primary density is the unevenness of the space of possible interactions.  
**Data:** Autocatalytic networks — "first survives." Triplet RNA interactions are biased toward cooperation due to catalytic constant distributions.

### 6. Physics (Honest Limit)
The model does not break, but adds no explanatory power beyond the existing formalism.  
To predict physical constants, we must determine which category describes physical reality — an open question.
